Privacy notice

This starter site records challenge answers in your current browser session only. If payment is enabled, Stripe processes payment information; this site does not store card details.

The server stores a payment session identifier, the email returned by the payment provider where available, a challenge score, issue date and certificate number to provide the purchased report and prevent duplicate certificate numbers.
